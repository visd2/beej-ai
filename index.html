Where the numbers come from

  • Nifty 50 (daily): daily closing prices from NSE (niftyindices.com), 2 Jan 2017 to 4 Nov 2026 built in, newer days can be added from the Time machine tab. Downloaded by the Beej owner. Price only, so dividends are not included. Used for the Time machine and the day-by-day chart.
  • Nifty 50 (yearly, with dividends): calendar-year returns of the UTI Nifty 50 Index Fund (growth, after costs), from integrato.in. Falls of 38% (2020) and 60% (2008) are from the same page.
  • Midcap 150: 2018 is the index total return from the NSE factsheet. 2019 (from 1 February) to 2025 are index fund returns from integrato.in. 2016 and 2017 were not found, so they are left out.
  • Gold: change in the yearly average 24K price in India, from Aditya Birla Capital. These are indicative figures, and 2025's +73% is unusual.
  • PPF: quarterly government rates from stableinvestor.com (updated 30 September 2026), averaged by calendar year by us.
  • Fixed deposit: SBI 1-year rate of 6.5% (16 May 2025) from GoldenPi, held constant. Gold's 15% fall is our assumption.

Fund returns are after costs. Tax is not included. Different sources use different bases (fund returns and index returns), so small gaps are expected. Figures were collected on 5 November 2026 and are not updated automatically.

What no pattern can do

No chart can find a choice that gives high returns with no risk. If one existed, everyone would buy it and the price would rise until the free lunch was gone. Anyone selling a sure high return is a warning sign. What Beej can do is show the range of outcomes, so you pick the one you can live with.

Planning tool, not investment advice. Beej is not a SEBI-registered adviser. Past results do not promise future ones, and these estimates replay history from 2018 to 2025. Taxes and bank rule changes are not included. Speak to a SEBI-registered adviser before you invest large amounts.
